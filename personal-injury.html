After a crash

What to do after an accident in Nevada

  1. Get to safety and call 911.
  2. Get medical care, even if you feel fine.
  3. Photograph vehicles, the scene and injuries.
  4. Get witness names and numbers.
  5. Don't give a recorded statement to the other insurer.
  6. Call Joey Gilbert Law: 775-210-1501.

Injury FAQ

How much does it cost to hire Joey Gilbert Law for an injury case?+

Injury cases are handled on contingency: no attorney fee unless we recover compensation for you.* Case costs are explained in your written fee agreement.

How long do I have to file an injury claim in Nevada?+

Nevada's general deadline for personal injury lawsuits is two years from the injury (NRS 11.190), with exceptions. Don't wait; evidence disappears fast.

Should I talk to the other driver's insurance company?+

Be careful. Adjusters may record statements and use them to reduce your claim. Call us first.

What is my case worth?+

It depends on medical bills, lost wages, pain and suffering, insurance limits and fault. We'll review it free. No one can honestly promise a number up front.
